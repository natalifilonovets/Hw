# Art generation record

Tool: built-in Imagegen, not the API/CLI fallback. All four backgrounds requested wide 16:9, no text/UI. Character requests used `transparent_background: true`. Final consumed assets are in `assets/`; no files in the tool output directory are referenced at runtime.

A luxurious whimsical 3D animated family movie still, warm inviting spooky atmosphere, intricate tactile materials, cinematic moonlight and pools of amber light. A continuous modern Halloween street with FIVE distinct charming crooked cottages spaced across the composition: orange pumpkin porch, mint ghost cottage, violet costume shop cottage, moss green werewolf garden cottage with bench and large pumpkin, red mystery prank cottage. All five houses fully visible, windows glowing, pumpkins, bunting, strings of lights, cobblestone winding path across foreground, indigo starry sky and crescent moon, richly layered trees. Camera wide and straight on. No text, no UI, no letters, no characters in foreground. Houses fill middle half vertically, leave sky at top and path foreground for UI overlays. Save output image as a project asset.
